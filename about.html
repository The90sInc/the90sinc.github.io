About Me

Hello, and welcome! I'm glad you stopped by.

If you're wondering who I am: depending on who you ask, I'm either a genius, a minor villain, or most accurately, an overly inquisitive nerd.

I've always been obsessed with how things work: cars, computers, robots, and complex systems. When I'm not reading fantasy novels (one of my favourite all time novels is Six of Crows) or watching Formula 1 (specifically rooting for Lewis Hamilton), I'm usually asking far too many questions.

My journey began in mechanical and automotive engineering with a B.Tech.Ed from Ekiti State University, which naturally spiraled into a fascination with robotics, autonomous systems, and AI. Over the years, that curiosity translated into real-world systems: I've built AI and Machine learning models for lending operations at Lendsqr, designed multimodal pipelines, and automated support workflows to cut down operational friction.

I also have a deep love for teaching. Breaking down complex subjects like Robot Operating System 2 (ROS2), control algorithms, or embedded systems—until the concept clicks for a student is one of the most rewarding things I do.

What gets me excited

  • Automotive Technology and Racing: The possibility of one day working with a major car manufacturer like BMW, or with an F1 team, applying AI and robotics techniques to the automotive industry.
  • Autonomous Driving and V2X: Vehicle-to-Everything communication and the future of connected mobility.
  • Cyber-Physical Systems: SLAM techniques, advanced control algorithms, and human ingenuity at its peak.
  • Applied AI: Translating raw data and complex models into functional products.

This site is a cumulative record of that journey: research builds, software projects, embedded experiments, and the occasional write-up. WELCOME!!!
