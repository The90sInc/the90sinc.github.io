About Me

Product specialist and data scientist with hands-on experience designing and deploying Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) solutions for lending operations, automation of support workflows, and multimodal data pipelines. Strong background in automotive technology, robotics, and applied machine learning with practical experience across product implementation, model development, and cross-functional training with a proven ability to reduce operational friction and improve decision-making through data-driven systems.

My path started in automotive and mechanical engineering — a B.Tech.Ed in Mechanical and Auto Mechanical Technology from Ekiti State University — before moving into robotics, AI, and product work. I've built credit models at a lending fintech, taught ROS2 and embedded systems as a course instructor, and shipped small robotics and computer-vision projects on the side. This site collects that work: research builds, software projects, and the odd write-up along the way.
